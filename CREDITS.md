A huge thank you to the **[OoT3D Randomizer team](https://github.com/gamestabled/OoT3D_Randomizer)** and everyone who has contributed to the project. Bringing that work into SariaUI would not have been possible without them.

The adapted randomizer source is included under `externals/oot3d-randomizer/`. Its [license and upstream acknowledgements](externals/oot3d-randomizer/LICENSE.md) are preserved, including credit to the original OoT Randomizer contributors.
